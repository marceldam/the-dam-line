Marcel Damian & The Craft

01 / Leadership

CEO OF THE DAM LINE

Led by Marcel Damian as CEO OF THE DAM LINE, the house approaches garment-making with architectural discipline—treating cut, proportion, fabric weight, and seam tension as permanent structural details rather than fleeting seasonal noise.

02 / Silhouette Lineup

THE BRAND

THE BRAND delivers structured jackets, wide-leg baggy trousers, extended knee-cut jogger jorts (with 50/50 LEFT marked right above the extended knee cut), knit polos, and structured caps—cut from dense weaves with clean drapes and intentional hardware.

03 / Why I Made It

Definition Over Hype

I created THE DAM LINE because too much modern clothing feels disposable, thin, and forgettable. I wanted pieces that hold their shape, feel substantial on the body, and carry unmistakable identity from the first wear.
